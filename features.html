FEATURES

Controls

  • Drag / swipe on the playfield to change direction
  • Tap anywhere on the canvas to pause / resume
  • Keyboard: Arrows or WASD · P / Esc to pause
  • Game opens in fullscreen when you hit Play
  • No on-screen d-pad — clean immersive play

Shop

  • 80+ skins with tiered powers (score / coins / combo)
  • 40+ backgrounds + custom image upload
  • 14 trails with live particle preview & powers
  • 23 food items — Apple (1 pt) to Neon Melon (150 pts)
  • Every skin, background, trail & food can grant power bonuses

Gameplay

  • Combo system — eat fast for × multipliers
  • Bonus orbs: ember, sunstone, frost, shield, power cells, diamonds, magnet…
  • 30+ maps, difficulty obstacles, optional hazards
  • Levels speed up · achievements & advancements
  • Coins earn from score · spend in shop · local save

Powers stack

  • Equipped skin + background + trail + food all add % score, % coins, or longer combo windows
